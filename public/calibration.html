Calibration · do A-grade stallions actually outperform?

For each letter grade, this page aggregates the realized performance signals across the stallions in that bucket. If the model is well-calibrated, each lower grade should show meaningfully lower realized pinhook lift, yearling ratio, and 2YO ratio than the next higher grade. If two adjacent grades converge, the cutoffs are conflating real differences.

This page is the model's accountability layer. It shows whether the score is doing the work it claims.
